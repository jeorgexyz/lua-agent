The two columns are the whole point.

The first column is the GRAMMAR's work: whether the output is a
syntactically valid tool call. Masking takes it to 100% on a model
that has never seen JSON.

The second column is the MODEL's work: whether the tool it chose could
plausibly serve the task. The grammar cannot help here, and a 15M
TinyStories checkpoint has nothing to contribute.

Tool calling is a decoding constraint. Choosing the right tool is not.
